FAQ

Before we meet

Do I need to speak Romanian?

Not at all. Lessons can be held fully in English, from the first one to the last.

Do I need my own guitar?

No. There are guitars in the studio you can use during lessons, so you can start without your own instrument. If you want to buy one, I can help you pick a model that fits your level and budget.

From what age can kids start?

Lessons are a good fit for kids from age 5 and up. For the little ones, the pace and materials are adapted to their age, and we work on songs they already know.

Do I need to know how to read music or any theory?

No previous knowledge needed. Lessons start straight from songs, and music theory comes in step by step, as it becomes useful.

How long is a lesson and how often?

A lesson is 60 minutes. Most students come once a week, but we set the frequency based on your availability and goals.

Where are the lessons held?

Lessons are in person, in the studio on Strada Matei Voievod 114, Sector 2, Bucharest. The schedule is flexible and includes weekends.
